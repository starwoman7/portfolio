here i am

Starwoman is a multidisciplinary artist bred, born, and raised in the Philippines. Known for a playful use of light and minimal equipment, she documents her life and the lives of others. Liminal spaces, mythmaking, nostalgia, queer culture, brutalism, the human body, and nature are common themes in her work. Currently, she studies at UP Diliman.
